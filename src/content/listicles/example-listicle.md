This is a sample post so you can see how a listicle looks on the site. Each numbered item is a level-two heading (`##`), which automatically builds the "In this list" box above and the structured data search engines read.

## 1. They agree on what "done" means

Before work starts, the team writes down what finished looks like. It sounds obvious, but it removes most of the back-and-forth at the end.

**Best for:** teams that keep reopening "finished" work.

## 2. They keep work small

Smaller pieces of work are easier to review, easier to test, and easier to undo. Big launches get split into steps that can each go live on their own.

## 3. They write things down once

Decisions, how-tos and context live in one place everyone can find, so nobody has to ask the same question twice.

## 4. They review quickly

A review that waits two days costs more than the review itself. Fast teams treat reviews as the first thing to do, not the last.

## 5. They look back and adjust

Every few weeks, the team asks what slowed them down and changes one thing. Small fixes add up.

## The bottom line

None of these habits needs a big budget. Pick one, try it for two weeks, and see what changes.
